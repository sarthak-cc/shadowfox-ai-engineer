# CS204 Operating Systems — Course Handbook (Fall 2026)

## Instructor and contact
The course is taught by Dr. Meera Iyer. Office hours are on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 3:00 pm to 4:30 pm in Room B-214. Questions about grading should be sent to the course email cs204-help@example.edu, not to the instructor's personal address.

## Assessment
The final grade is made up of four parts:
- Weekly quizzes: 15%. The lowest two quiz scores are dropped.
- Programming assignments: 35%. There are five assignments, each worth 7%.
- Midterm exam: 20%, held in week 8.
- Final exam: 30%. The final exam is closed-book, but students may bring one A4 sheet of handwritten notes (both sides).

A student must score at least 40% on the final exam to pass the course, regardless of their overall total.

## Late submission policy
Each student has three "grace days" for the whole semester that can be used on programming assignments without penalty. After the grace days are used up, late work loses 10% per day, and work more than three days late receives zero. Grace days cannot be used for quizzes or exams.

## Academic integrity
Assignments are individual. Discussing ideas is allowed, but sharing code is not. All submissions are checked with a similarity tool. A first offence results in zero for the assignment; a second offence is reported to the academic integrity committee.

## Attendance
Attendance in lab sessions is mandatory. Missing more than three lab sessions without a medical certificate results in a 5% deduction from the final grade.
